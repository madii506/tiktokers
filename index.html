how it works

  1. one line

    Describe your TikToker. It becomes a fictional adult creator with its own face, made with FLUX.

  2. a trend

    Pick one of eight trends and it films a real 5-second vertical AI video. Your first test videos are free.

  3. a coin

    Name it and launch it on pump.fun from your own wallet. The name becomes the ticker.

  4. a new video every 6 hours

    While the coin trades, its TikToker films the next trend. Download it and post it on the coin’s TikTok.

what is TIKTOKERS?

A launchpad where every coin comes with an AI TikToker: a fictional creator that films short vertical videos for it. You launch on pump.fun; the TikToker keeps filming new trends while the coin trades.

does it post on TikTok for me?

No. TikTok doesn’t let apps open accounts, so you make the coin’s TikTok account yourself in a minute. Every video has a download button and a ready caption; you post it. Turn on TikTok’s AI-generated label when you do.

how are the videos made?

The face is made with FLUX. The videos are image-to-video from that face, made with Seedance (Wan as the backup) through Vercel’s AI Gateway. Captions come from a small OpenAI model. Every video is 5 seconds, vertical, silent.

who gets the fees?

pump.fun’s own fee sharing splits every creator fee, locked in the same signatures that launch the coin: 70% to you, 30% to the house, which pays for every video. Before you sign, the page reads the transactions and refuses to go on if the split isn’t the one shown.

how often does it film?

One video right after launch, then one every six hours while the coin trades. A day without a trade and it slows down; seven days and it stops. The house has a daily video budget; if it runs out, filming resumes at 00:00 UTC.

can I make a real person?

No. Every TikToker is a fictional adult. Ideas that ask for a real or famous person, a minor or anything sexual are refused before a photo is made. There are no uploads of real faces.

is this TikTok?

No. TIKTOKERS is independent and not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.

does TIKTOKERS hold my keys?

No. You sign everything in your own wallet and the server has no key. The house is a public address; its share arrives through pump.fun’s split.
